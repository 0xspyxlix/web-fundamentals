Who I Am

Hey, I'm Awad. I grew up in Qatar and I'm currently a student at Lipscomb University. When I'm not in class I'm usually messing around with cybersecurity stuff or kicking a ball around on the soccer field. I got into travel mostly because growing up in Doha you're surrounded by people from everywhere, and hearing their stories made me want to go see those places for myself.

I wouldn't say I'm the most experienced traveler, but I've been to a few countries now and every trip teaches me something. I started this blog to keep track of where I've been and where I want to go next. It's also a project for my web development class so it doubles as practice.

Outside of traveling and school, I do bug bounty hunting which is basically finding security flaws on websites and getting paid for reporting them. It's kind of like treasure hunting but digital. Between that and soccer, I stay pretty busy.
