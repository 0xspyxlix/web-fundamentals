Bucket List Destinations

These are the places I want to visit at some point. Some of them are realistic for the next year or two and others are more long term goals, but they're all on the list.

  • Japan - Tokyo and Kyoto especially, I want to see the mix of old and modern
  • Iceland - the northern lights and the volcanic landscape look unreal
  • Spain - Barcelona for the architecture and the food
  • New Zealand - hiking and the scenery from Lord of the Rings
  • Morocco - it's close to home culturally and I've heard Marrakech is incredible
  • Switzerland - the mountains and trains, plus it just looks peaceful
