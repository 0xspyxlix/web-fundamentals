How I Plan a Trip

I used to just book a flight and figure out the rest when I got there, which honestly worked out sometimes but also led to a couple of nights where I had nowhere to sleep. So now I actually plan things out a bit more. Not like a full spreadsheet or anything but at least a rough idea of what I'm doing each day.

Tip: book accommodation for at least the first two nights before you land. Everything else you can figure out on the ground, but having somewhere to go when you're tired from flying makes a huge difference.

My Go-To Packing List

  1. Passport and copies of important documents
  2. Phone charger and a portable battery pack
  3. One week of clothes even for longer trips (just do laundry)
  4. A good pair of walking shoes
  5. Basic toiletries in travel-size containers
  6. A light jacket no matter how warm the forecast says

Budget Breakdown

For a week-long trip I usually try to keep it under $1500 total. That covers flights, a hostel or budget hotel, food, and activities. It's tight but doable if you're not eating out at expensive places every night. Street food is almost always better anyway.
