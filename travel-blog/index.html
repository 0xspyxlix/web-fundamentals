My First Trip

The first time I actually left Qatar on my own was a trip to Turkey when I was 16. My parents were nervous about it but I had been saving up for months and I had a family friend in Istanbul who said I could stay with them. It was one of those trips where everything goes slightly wrong but you still have an amazing time anyway. I missed my connecting flight, my phone died at the airport, and I ended up wandering around the terminal for two hours before figuring out the free wifi situation.

"The real voyage of discovery consists not in seeking new landscapes, but in having new eyes." - Marcel Proust

Trip Planning Steps

  1. Pick where you actually want to go and figure out if you need a visa
  2. Set a budget and start saving - flights and hotels add up fast
  3. Book your flights and accommodation at least a month ahead
  4. Make a rough itinerary but leave room for spontaneous stuff
  5. Pack light - you always think you need more than you do
