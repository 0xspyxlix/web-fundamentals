Traveling

Why I Love It

Traveling is probably my favorite thing to do outside of school and tech stuff. I grew up in Qatar and moved to the US for college, so I have always been around different cultures. There is something about landing in a new city and just walking around with no plan that I find really relaxing. You end up finding spots that no travel guide mentions.

My favorite trips so far have been to Turkey and a few places around the Gulf. I am trying to make it to Japan and Spain at some point. Every trip teaches you something about yourself and the world that you cannot get from reading about it.

If you want to start planning trips, check out Lonely Planet for destination guides and travel tips.
